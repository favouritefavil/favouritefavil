# 📁 Featured Credit Risk Projects

### 🏢 [Dangote Cement Plc — Enterprise Credit Risk Assessment](https://github.com/favouritefavil/dangote-cement-enterprise-credit-risk-assessment)

*Corporate Credit Assessment · Financial Statement Analysis · Credit Committee Simulation · Power BI*

* Conducted a four-year FY2022–FY2025 enterprise credit assessment using audited financial statements to evaluate leverage, liquidity, cash-flow generation, repayment capacity, FX exposure, and contingent liabilities.
* Identified substantial deleveraging, with total debt declining from approximately **₦2.51T to ₦1.08T**, Debt/EBITDA improving from **1.82x to 0.55x**, and interest coverage recovering to **5.10x**, while sub-1x liquidity and residual FX/contingent-liability risks remained relevant.
* Produced a **Credit Committee-style appraisal** and a **5-page Power BI Early Warning / credit-monitoring dashboard** to support ongoing risk assessment.

### 🏠 [Mortgage Delinquency Risk Analysis](https://github.com/favouritefavil/Mortgage-Delinquency-Risk-Analysis)

*Fannie Mae · 84.18M Records · SQL · Power BI*

* Built an **8-stage SQL processing pipeline** across 84.18M loan-month observations to produce a 1.92M-loan latest-snapshot portfolio for delinquency surveillance.
* Identified a **22.8% delinquency rate among modified loans versus 0.6% for non-modified loans**, highlighting a substantial difference in observed delinquency behaviour.
* Developed a **6-page Power BI portfolio-surveillance dashboard** covering borrower, geographic, delinquency, and exposure-risk views.

### 🏦 [Microfinance Portfolio Risk Analysis](https://github.com/favouritefavil/Microfinance-Portfolio-Performance-Analysis)

*₦271.6M Portfolio · 3,922 Loans · PostgreSQL · Power BI*

* Analysed portfolio quality using **PAR 30/60/90, NPL, collection performance, default behaviour, and vintage analysis** across regional and borrower segments.
* Identified a **14.6% default rate versus a 10% benchmark**, a **4.2x lower default rate among repeat/Cycle 5 borrowers**, and a loan-officer capacity level approximately **765% of assigned capacity**.
* Developed a **3-page Power BI portfolio-monitoring framework** to support regional, borrower, and operational risk oversight.

### 💳 [LendingClub — Loan Portfolio Risk Monitoring](https://github.com/favouritefavil/Loan-Portfolio-Risk-Monitoring-Credit-Risk-Analysis)

*1.34M Loans · ~$19B Exposure · SQL · Power BI*

* Performed SQL-based portfolio segmentation across credit grades, loan purpose, borrower characteristics, and historical origination cohorts.
* Identified approximately **$4B in gross loss exposure**, including approximately **$1.3B associated with Grade C loans** and **$2.7B associated with debt-consolidation loans**.
* Built a **Power BI portfolio-monitoring framework** demonstrating why default frequency should be assessed alongside dollar-loss concentration when evaluating portfolio risk.

### 💰 [Cashflow-Based Credit Scoring Model](https://github.com/favouritefavil/CASHFLOW-BASED-CREDIT-RISK-MODEL)

*2,000 Borrowers · Alternative Credit Assessment · Python · Excel*

* Engineered transaction-level features covering **cash-flow stability, liquidity, financial pressure, and income behaviour** for an enterprise-simulated credit assessment framework.
* Combined a **weighted scorecard and Logistic Regression** approach, achieving **100% agreement between the two methods for Low- and High-risk classifications**.
* Developed a **6-sheet interactive Excel credit-decision tool** supporting consistent borrower assessment and approve/reject/review decisions.

### 📊 [Credit Default Risk Modeling — German Credit](https://github.com/favouritefavil/Credit-Default-Risk-Modeling-Using-German-Credit-Data)

*1,000 Applicants · Probability of Default · PostgreSQL · Python*

* Developed an interpretable **Logistic Regression Probability of Default model** with an **AUC of approximately 0.74**.
* Identified compound risk among thin-file and low-savings borrowers, with observed default rates of approximately **70–72% versus a 30% baseline** within the analysed segments.
* Applied **cost-sensitive threshold optimization**, reducing missed defaults by approximately **55%** and translating the modelling result into credit-policy implications.
